Privacy Policy

Welcome to TWOZYER! We value your privacy and believe in complete transparency regarding how your data is handled on our multi-business platform.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our services, we collect your email address, a custom username (real names are not required), UPI ID, wallet balance, and your deposit, withdrawal, and transaction history.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your financial data is securely sent to our admin panel to verify fractional decimal payments. This ensures that your deposits, withdrawals, and platform transactions remain fully accurate and operational.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Apps

Because TWOZYER hosts multiple applications, your data is never sold to outside ad agencies. Your relevant information (such as your username, UPI ID, and balance) is shared only with the specific third-party business owner whose app you are actively using.

4. Data Security & Tracking

Your data is stored safely in our Supabase cloud database, which acts as a secure vault. We do not track your specific device or IP address. We only utilize Local Storage to maintain basic login sessions.

5. Auto-Deletion & Your Rights

You retain full control over your account and can delete it at any time. To keep our systems clean, if your account remains inactive for 7 continuous months, our system will automatically delete your account and all associated data permanently, without any prior notification.

6. Age Restrictions

TWOZYER does not enforce a platform-wide age restriction. However, individual third-party apps hosted on our platform may implement their own age limitations based on their specific products, which users must follow.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding your privacy, data security, or need support, please contact us at: twozyer@gmail.com.
