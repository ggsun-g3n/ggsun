Privacy Policy

Welcome! We value your privacy and want to be completely transparent about how we handle your data in a simple and direct way.

What Data We Collect & How

We only collect your username and password. This data securely travels from our website to our Supabase database, and then to our Minecraft plugin.

Why We Collect It

We use this data strictly to verify your identity on our Minecraft server, ensuring the account belongs to a real human. This helps us safely whitelist your account and keep both our database and Minecraft server protected.

Data Security

Your security is our top priority. All passwords are Hash Encrypted before reaching the Minecraft server. This means they are completely hidden—not even our developers can see your password.

Third-Party Services

To keep things running smoothly, we use trusted and secure services: GitHub (for our website structure), Supabase (for our database), and Lordhost (for server hosting).

Your Rights & Auto-Deletion

You have full control and can change your own password at any time. To keep our database clean, if you do not play on the server for a period of 6 months to 1 year, your data will be automatically deleted.

Children's Privacy

Our system is highly protected. We intentionally do not collect any sensitive personal information, making it completely safe for younger players.

Policy Updates

If we ever make changes to this policy, we will directly notify you in our Discord server.
